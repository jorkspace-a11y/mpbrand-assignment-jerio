## Boundaries

- Founder layouts and prompts are proposals, not attributed quotations. An approved portrait or real interview is needed for production.
- Source reviews cover the sampled frames and linked scenes actually inspected, not every clip or all 176 tour views.
- Tour capture labels are reported as shown, not independently verified construction dates.
- Quotation #578 scope and prices stay unchanged. Conflicting dates, teaser duration and source legal spelling remain explicit pre-issue checks.
- Mockups are not issued invoices, publication-ready campaigns or a promise of performance.
- Real supplied project images and native graphic shapes are used. No generated people, synthetic project photography or decorative author watermark.

## Reviewer journey

The library keeps wider experiments available without repeating their rationale in the final response.
