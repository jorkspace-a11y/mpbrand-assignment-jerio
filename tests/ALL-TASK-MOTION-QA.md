# Every-page motion / 15 September 2026

The user requested automatic, interactive motion throughout the assignment, not just the home and company landing pages.

## Coverage

Twelve task-specific studies plus the library, full logo applications and quotation creator use `task-motion.js`. The landing task retains its existing contour-lips preview; the assignment homepage and three company landing identities retain their existing kinetic artwork. Every public page family therefore has automatic motion and usable controls.

The new studies use apertures, ribbons, mark tracks, colour bars, fifteen-frame sequences, exact 4:5 / 9:16 / 16:9 frames, abstract conversation rhythm, shutters, six intersecting territories, crop brackets, supplied tour scenes and layered document forms. Controls update the visual study, pressed state and caption. Support-page links lead to actual artwork, applications and form controls.

- Each of the 15 phone-sized studies visually inspected. Varied desktop studies inspected separately, plus fresh-eyes code and screenshot review.
- `submission-preflight.js`: 17 routes and 77 download links passed. Existing frame geometry and emoji-capable UI-symbol checks passed.
- Exact application frame proportions checked at 390 and 1440. Six quotation price combinations, escaping of client input and reset behaviour passed.
- Existing PDF and campaign/application binaries were not changed. New interactive studies are screen-only, and original phase-head print dimensions are restored.

## Limits

These are Chromium viewport checks, not physical iPhone/Safari certification or an aesthetic sign-off. Offscreen and hidden-tab suspension are implemented in the native controller. There is no new animation dependency, invented founder audio, substituted portrait, borrowed reference artwork or change to quotation source amounts.

Evidence lives outside the site in the original workspace's `output/playwright/motion-study-*` files. `tests/all-task-motion.js` can run against the local or public origin of the currently open page.
